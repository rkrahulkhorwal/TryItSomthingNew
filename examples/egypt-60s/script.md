# Hook
Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.
That is how old Egypt is.

# Two Lands, One King [card: 3100 BC]
Around 3100 BC, a king named Narmer united Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom. {NAME: NARMER}
Every summer the Nile flooded, and the black mud it left behind fed a whole civilization.

# The Pyramid Age [card: 2560 BC]
Pharaohs were treated as living gods, and gods need very big tombs.
The Great Pyramid used about 2.3 million stone blocks. {NUMBER: 2.3 MILLION BLOCKS}
And archaeologists think it was built by paid workers, not slaves.

# Empire [card: 1550 BC]
Then foreign kings seized the north, riding a terrifying new weapon: the horse-drawn chariot.
So Egypt copied the chariots, kicked the invaders out, and built an empire.
Ramesses the Great ruled for 66 years. {NAME: RAMESSES II}

# The Long Goodbye [card: 30 BC]
But empires get tired.
Libyans, Nubians, Persians and Greeks took turns on the throne.
The last ruling pharaoh was Cleopatra, | and when she died, Rome took Egypt. {NAME: CLEOPATRA}

# Lost and Found
Within a few centuries, nobody on Earth could read hieroglyphs.
For fourteen centuries, Egypt's writing was a total mystery.
Until one stone with three scripts cracked the code, | and we finally learned how old Egypt really is. {LABEL: ROSETTA STONE}
